The thermodynamic price of a consistent time loop

What does Novikov self-consistency cost? In solvable stochastic-thermodynamics models: exactly the Boltzmann weight of what the loop must preserve — and the bill attaches to records, not to time travel itself.

Series: follow-up to Report I (the full claim ledger) · quantifies thesis T3 · pre-registered targets in docs/P1_TARGET.md, committed before results (commit f039282) · code: p1/run_all.py in the public repo.

Abstract. Report I found that self-consistent time-loop histories are generic rather than rare — yet Kevin's original "almost all time travel is impossible" retained a defensible core (T3): macroscopic pastward intervention should be entropically suppressed. Here we make that quantitative in explicit models. For a dissipative system threading a trajectory-level (Novikov) time loop, with Markovian detailed-balance dynamics: (1) the probability of consistent closure decays monotonically with loop duration to the Boltzmann–Einstein weight of the loop state — e−ΔF/kBT, i.e. e−ΔS/kB for an entropy-deficit state: short hops are cheap, loops longer than the relaxation time pay the full price of the loop state's persistence. (2) Around an undriven consistent loop, entropy production is not merely suppressed — it is exactly zero, path by path (2,187 of 2,187 enumerated loops); with external driving, a detailed fluctuation theorem holds inside the consistent ensemble, so loops obey the second law statistically and cannot be free-lunch machines. (3) A loop persistently hosting free-energy excess ΔF closes with probability e−ΔF/kBT — no delivery of free energy to the past except at full Boltzmann tax. (4) The price is extensive: independent components multiply, giving ~10−2.6×10²² for one conspiratorial gram — Report I's scale estimate, now derived. (5) Quantum contrast: Deutsch density-matrix consistency is satisfiable at every tested coupling with no thermodynamic gate, while pointer-level "record" consistency reproduces the classical exponential suppression — the thermodynamic price of time travel attaches to memory. Together these give "almost all macroscopic time travel doesn't happen" a theorem-grade meaning inside a declared model class — while confirming it can never be strengthened to "impossible."

Per the lab's method, three research agents were tasked to refute the novelty of the planned results before we invested in them. What the scan found:

Physics sweep. The closest prior art is Devin (arXiv:1302.3298, 2013) — a qubit time machine with channel error rate k, a work-extraction bound W ≲ −ln k, and an assembly scaling keff = kN: recognizable germs of our T4, with "exponentially difficult" rhetoric for time tourists. It contains no stochastic dynamics, no fluctuation theorem, no closure-equals-return-probability statement, and dismisses the Deutsch comparison; thirteen years on it has only self-citations. Rovelli (arXiv:1912.04702, 2019) states our T1/T2a punchline at essay level — dS/dτ must vanish around a loop, no memory survives, pastward travel is "extremely improbable" — with no formula, model, or exponent (it also anticipated Report I's memory corollary, for which we have issued a credit adjustment). Gavassino (CQG 2025) reaches exact consistency by quantum kinematics (energy-level discretization forces every system on a CTC back to its initial state; memories erased) — consistency enforced, never priced. Lossev & Novikov (CQG 1992), abstract verified verbatim, identified the thermodynamic maintenance requirement of closed-worldline objects ("Jinn"): "These systems are possible only if they can, by interacting with external objects, gain energy to regenerate their internal structure" — qualitative; our T1/C3 supply that requirement's price. The quantum-information genre exploits CTCs against the second law (Bartkiewicz et al., PRA 2019: circuits "which lead to decrease of entropy"; Lloyd et al.'s P-CTCs), assuming consistency for free; Cassidy & Hawking (1998) showed entropy suppresses forming a CTC region — a different object; Mikheeva & Novikov (1993) showed dissipative billiards still admit consistent solutions — existence, never probability. Structural negatives: an INSPIRE query for "closed timelike" AND "second law" returns zero records; sweeps for fluctuation theorem × CTC, Jarzynski/Crooks × CTC, Landauer × CTC found nothing combining stochastic thermodynamics with Novikov consistency.

Philosophy sweep. The improbability argument is Horwich's (1987, restated 1995), from the bilking lineage of Black (1956) and Dummett (1964), debated by Smith (1997), Dowe (2003), Sider (2002), Ismael (2003) — every side arguing with informal probability only; Sider's restatement concedes "a rash of coincidences would apparently not violate any law." 3 · Setup

Classical model class. A finite, irreducible Markov chain (discrete or continuous time) with transition rates w(x→y) > 0 obeying local detailed balance with a single heat bath at temperature T; stationary distribution π is Gibbs. The loop is a trajectory-level Novikov consistency condition: an ideal junction returns the loop variable's microstate from coordinate time τ to time 0, so a history is consistent iff x(0) = x(τ) = x (event Cx). The medium entropy of a path γ is s(γ) = kB Σ ln[w(xi→xi+1)/w(xi+1→xi)] (heat delivered to the bath over T).

Quantum comparison. The Deutsch condition ρ = Φ(ρ) ("state-level consistency") versus record consistency — the pointer-basis value must return around the loop.

Assumptions, fixed in advance (ledger entries A-P1-1…3): the junction adds no dissipation of its own; probabilities are statements in the ordinary path measure of the dynamics (the same typicality reading as any statistical-mechanics claim about our one world); bath undriven except where stated. Everything below is conditional on this class — the standard honesty rail of this lab.

4 · Results

Theorem 1 — the closure cost held · E1 + E2

T1. For reversible chains the closure probability Rτ(x) = P(x(τ)=x | x(0)=x) is monotonically decreasing in τ, bounded below by π(x), and converges to π(x) on the relaxation timescale. For a macrostate M, Rτ(M) → π(M) = e−(FM−Feq)/kBT — for states distinguished by entropy alone, e−(Seq−SM)/kB (the Einstein fluctuation weight).

Why. A reversible generator is self-adjoint in the π-weighted inner product, so Pτ(x,x) = π(x) + Σk≥2 e−λkτ φk(x)² π(x) with all λk > 0: a sum of decaying exponentials on top of the stationary weight — hence monotone, hence ≥ π(x), hence → π(x). The macrostate limit is the stationary measure of M, which at temperature T is its Boltzmann weight. ∎

Physical reading: a jump five minutes back, closed quickly, costs almost nothing — R ≈ 1. A loop that outlives the system's relaxation time pays the full Boltzmann price of keeping the loop state in existence. The often-quoted intuition "the universe forbids time travel" becomes, in-model, the tamer and sharper statement: the universe charges Boltzmann rates for it.

Theorem 2 — the second law inside the loop held · E1 + E2, sharpened

T2a (undriven — sharper than pre-registered). With a single bath and no driving, every consistent loop has exactly zero net medium entropy production: s(γ) = 0 identically, not merely on average.
T2b (driven). With external driving, within the consistent ensemble the detailed fluctuation theorem P(s | C)/P(−s | C) = es/k_B holds; consequently ⟨e−s/k_B⟩C = 1, P(s ≤ −σ | C) ≤ e−σ/k_B, and ⟨s⟩C ≥ 0.

Why. (a) Undriven, each jump's log-ratio is −(Ey−Ex)/T, so the sum telescopes to (Ex(0)−Ex(τ))/T — zero on any periodic path. (b) Time-reversal is a bijection of the periodic ensemble onto itself (same pinned endpoint), and the path-probability ratio of a trajectory to its reverse is es/k_B; the conditioning weights cancel, giving the conditional DFT; the integral form and the tail bound follow by summation, and ⟨s⟩C ≥ 0 by Jensen. ∎

Physical reading: T2a is Report I's leaf C2a — "closed worldlines must be perfectly reversible" — surviving contact with an exact model as an identity, not an approximation: an undriven consistent loop exchanges zero net heat, so any dissipative episode inside the loop must be exactly un-dissipated elsewhere along it. The improbability of that un-dissipation is not an extra charge — it is Theorem 1's closure cost; the books close. T2b says driving doesn't open a loophole: consistent loops are statistically ordinary — an entropy-consuming loop is exponentially rare in exactly the way an entropy-consuming ordinary process is. Time loops are not perpetual-motion machines.

Corollary 3 — no free energy to the past held · E1

C3. A loop whose cargo persistently carries free-energy excess ΔF above the bath's equilibrium closes with probability → e−ΔF/kBT. A time loop can host a charged battery — only at the full Boltzmann tax on its charge.

Why. Immediate from Theorem 1: the stationary weight of a state with free-energy excess ΔFx = Fx − Feq is exactly e−ΔFx/kBT. ∎ The "bilking" scenario — using the loop to gift your past self a resource — is thereby priced, not paradoxed.

Theorem 4 — the price is extensive held · E1 + E2

T4. Independent loop-threading components multiply: RN = (R₁)N = e−N·δ/k_B. The suppression of a macroscopic consistent traveler is exponential in its size.

Result 5 — where the price attaches held · E2 (numeric)

R5. Deutsch (density-matrix) consistency carries no thermodynamic gate: the consistency map has a fixed point at every coupling tested, reached from arbitrary initial states. Demanding instead that a record — a pointer-basis value — return around the loop reproduces the classical exponential suppression of Theorem 1. The thermodynamic price of a time loop attaches to records, not to the loop.

Physical reading: this is the quantitative form of Report I's corollary that "nothing can remember completing a closed loop." A quantum state may thread a loop freely — consistency is a fixed-point condition that costs nothing (and, in our model, the selected fixed point is the thermal state: the loop thermalizes). What is exponentially taxed is classicality around the loop: any pointer-like, copyable, rememberable configuration pays Boltzmann. A time machine for quantum information is thermodynamically cheap; a time machine for history — records, memories, travelers who know they traveled — is the thing nature prices out.

6 · Scope, objections, and what would break this

  • Model class. Everything is proven for Markovian local-detailed-balance dynamics with an ideal loop junction. A dissipative junction adds cost (strengthening the conclusion); non-Markovian baths and strongly interacting components (beyond T4's independence) are stated extensions, not covered claims.
  • The block-universe objection. "There is no ensemble of universes for these probabilities to live in." Our probabilities are typicality statements in the ordinary path measure — precisely the status of every statistical-mechanics claim about the single actual world. If that reading is rejected, the second law itself is rejected with it.
  • Reading of consistency. Trajectory-level Novikov is the strongest classical reading; Result 5 shows the conclusion inverts under Deutsch's weakest reading — that contrast is a finding, not a loophole.
  • No spacetime dynamics. Nothing here bears on whether loops can form (chronology protection, C4b). We price the ride, not the road.
  • Ingredients are standard. Spectral return probabilities, path-ratio fluctuation identities, and Jensen bounds are textbook stochastic thermodynamics. The contribution — per the novelty scan in §2 — is the assembly: pricing Novikov consistency with them, the conditional-ensemble framing, the exact T2a identity, and the state-vs-record split.
  • Status. Unrefereed, single-team work produced with an AI research partner; the novelty claim is exactly as good as the §2 scan. The natural next step is external review — a condensed version an expert can check in an afternoon.

8 · Methods

E1: two-state chain, closed-form Rτ (analytic), monotonicity checked pointwise. E2: exact enumeration of all periodic paths — undriven 3-state Metropolis chain (2,187 loops) and driven 3-ring at affinity 1.5 kBT per hop (6,561 loops); no sampling, no fits. E3: closed-form product. E4: 2-qubit partial-swap thermalizing map; Deutsch iteration from random states, 300 steps; record channel = the same map dephased in the pointer basis. All code: p1/run_all.py (numpy/matplotlib via uv); outputs committed in p1/out/; figures render in light and dark from a CVD-validated palette.
